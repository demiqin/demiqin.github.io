<div class="home-section">
  <h3>Research</h3>
  <p>My research sits at the intersection of <strong>machine learning (ML)</strong>, <strong>topological data analysis (TDA)</strong>, and <strong>visualization</strong>. I build methods that help us better understand complex data at scale, with applications spanning scalar fields, images, 3D shapes, and graphs.</p>
  <p>More broadly, I’m interested in how geometric and topological structure can make large-scale data analysis more reliable, interpretable, and useful for scientific discovery and decision-making, especially in scientific and energy systems.</p>
